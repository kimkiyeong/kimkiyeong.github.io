Personal AI

A private, single-user assistant. Not a public service.

What it does

  • Adds the owner's own task deadlines to a Google Calendar that this app created.
  • Stores encrypted backups of the owner's own data in files this app created in the owner's Google Drive.

Only the owner uses this app. It requests the narrowest Google permissions available: calendars and events it creates (calendar.app.created) and Drive files it creates (drive.file).
