Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Personal AI is used by a single person, its owner, to manage the owner's own data.

Google data accessed

  • Google Calendar: only calendars and events this app created, to show task deadlines.
  • Google Drive: only files this app created, to store encrypted backups.

How it is used

Data is used only to provide these features to the owner. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models. Backups are encrypted before upload.

Retention and deletion

Data stays in the owner's own Google account. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
